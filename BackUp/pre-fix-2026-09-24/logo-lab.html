Wavefy — logo directions

Four marks, each shown animated at full size and then at the sizes the app actually uses — 64px on a desktop header, 44px on a phone, 28px in a list — on the light tile as well as on a dark one, because a gradient that works on cream can disappear on charcoal.

Tick one and tell me, or just name the letter. The app icon PNGs get regenerated from whichever you choose.

A — Head & note

current mark, now animated

Your traced silhouette with the note cut out. Waves leave the note itself, the glyph breathes, and both speed up while audio plays. Strongest at large sizes; at 28px it becomes a solid blob with a hole.

B — Wave monogram

a W drawn as one waveform

A W built from a single continuous line, drawn and then unwound on a loop. It survives every size on this page — the stroke is what carries it, so shrinking removes detail instead of destroying the shape.

C — Groove ring

a record turning

Two dashed grooves rotating around a held note. The motion is one transform on one group with the subject still, which is both the cheapest animation here and the most literal: records turn.

D — Equaliser

the mark is the animation

Four bars dancing on the Y axis only. It is the most obviously musical and the most legible at 28px, and because the bars scale rather than resize, the mark keeps its exact silhouette at rest — the animation is additive, not structural.
